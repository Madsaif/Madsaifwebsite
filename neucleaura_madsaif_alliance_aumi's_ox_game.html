About This Game

OX is a modern take on the classic Tic Tac Toe game, built with precision and care by Maruf Karim Siddik Aumi — CEO & Researcher at Neucleaura. This game brings the timeless fun of Tic Tac Toe to your fingertips with a clean, responsive interface and smooth gameplay.

This page is published as part of a helping agreement between MadSaif and Neucleaura, showcasing the collaborative spirit of innovation. Whether you're challenging a friend on the same device or battling it out via Bluetooth, OX delivers a delightful experience for players of all ages.

With its minimal design and smart AI (in single-player mode), OX is perfect for quick matches during breaks, family game nights, or competitive sessions with friends. The game tracks your wins, losses, and draws, so you can always measure your progress.

Key Features

  • Classic Tic Tac Toe (OX) gameplay
  • Local 2‑Player — pass & play on one device
  • Bluetooth Multiplayer — challenge nearby friends wirelessly
  • Smart AI opponent for solo practice
  • Clean, distraction‑free interface
  • Match history & win/loss tracking
  • Light & dark themes for comfortable play
  • Offline‑ready — no internet needed
